The companion app works. `android/companion/` is built, installed on p8a, and

AVRCP → our MediaSession → mpv IPC → mpv, verified against a four-entry

Read `android/companion/README.md` first; it is short and current. The design

## What is being built

The app requests **audio focus** on mpv's behalf and translates focus changes
into mpv IPC commands. mpv ignores focus, and that one fact is the root of most
phone-side complexity we carry: `call_guard` polling notifications for calls,
the Automate mic-detect hold flag (broken since 2026-08-05), the duck/restore
plumbing. This is the step that starts retiring them.

`src/net/agentmedia/companion/FocusPolicy.java` is committed as a **starting
point, not a finished piece**: the decision table is written, it is wired to
nothing, and it has no tests. Treat it as a proposal in code — change it freely.

The shape it assumes:

**David's rule, which supersedes the table below where they disagree: duck the
music, pause the speech.** A half-heard sentence is a lost sentence, so speech
cannot be ducked meaningfully; music under a nav prompt or someone else's
podcast just needs to get out of the way. Apply it per channel rather than
treating both the same.

That is not a new policy — it is exactly what `call_guard` already does on a
hold (`_DEFAULT_SOCKET_NAMES` pauses `sink-speech.sock`, the duck list lowers
`mpv-music.sock`). Converge on it rather than inventing a second policy in the
app; the difference is only the trigger.

| Focus change | Music | Speech | Owed afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LOSS` | pause | pause | nothing — Android's convention is no auto-resume, and music restarting minutes later is worse than a button press |
| `LOSS_TRANSIENT` | duck | pause | unduck / resume on `GAIN` |
| `LOSS_TRANSIENT_CAN_DUCK` | duck to 15 | pause | restore the previous volume on `GAIN` |
| `GAIN` | unduck | resume if we paused it | — |

Pausing speech from the app needs **a second loopback bridge for the speech
socket**, the way music got one — `sink-speech.sock` is a separate Termux mpv
(6602 on the tailnet). Another `mpv-music-bridge-local`-shaped service, bound
to 127.0.0.1.

### "This used to work" — what is actually missing

David remembers music ducking and speech pausing when something else plays, and
asked what changed. As far as the evidence goes: **that behaviour was never
triggered by another app playing.** It is `call_guard`'s hold path, and it fires
on an incoming call or on the external-hold flag (Automate mic-detect, or a Cece
arbitration hold). The flag last fired 2026-08-13 14:38 with source `external`,
so the mechanism is alive, and nothing in `call_guard` or the coordinator
regressed — the recent commits there retire the dead `mpv-voice` lane and tighten
hold expiry.

What has never existed is a trigger for "another app is using the speakers".
That is precisely what audio focus provides, which is why this step delivers the
behaviour he is describing rather than restoring it.

Two recent things made it *feel* broken, and both are worth knowing before
chasing ghosts:

- **The volume clamp** (fixed in `c2db694`). Every duck restore clamped to 100
  against a service default of 130, so music got quieter after every spoken
  sentence and never came back.
- **The app pauses on any dispatched pause and owes nothing back.** With no
  focus policy wired, `MediaSession.Callback.onPause` sets `pause=true` on mpv
  and nothing ever resumes it. Observed live: music paused mid-test and stayed
  paused. The focus work replaces this with a policy that remembers what it did.

### Guards

Two guards matter more than the table. A resume from anywhere else (earbuds,
CLI, red5) must cancel a resume we owe, so we never fight the listener. And if
the volume moves somewhere we did not put it, something else owns it now — drop
the restore rather than clobber it. `call_guard` is still live and ducks the
same mpv during calls, so that case is real, not hypothetical.

Focus should follow `loaded()`, the same predicate the silent `AudioTrack`
uses: hold it while a file is open, including while paused. Abandoning focus on
our own pause would mean never receiving the `GAIN` that tells us to resume.

## How to work on this

- `./test/run.sh` — host-side tests against a fake mpv, seconds, no device.
  `Json`, `MpvIpc`, `MpvState` and `FocusPolicy` import nothing from
  `android.*` precisely so this works. **Put the focus decision table under
  test here before sideloading anything.** p8a has no adb; everything not
  proven on the host is debugged by squinting at a phone screen.
- `./build.sh` — a ~28 KB APK, no Gradle, no AndroidX, nothing from Maven.
  Keep `debug.keystore`; a different signing key forces an uninstall.
- Sideload: `scp` to `p8a:~/storage/downloads/`, then David taps through
  `termux-open --chooser`. **Ask before touching the phone.**
- The app's own screen is the only readout. `transport:` lines are session
  callbacks, `broadcast:` lines are the raw media-button path.

## Things that will bite

- **This working tree is shared with other sessions that `git reset --hard
  origin/main`.** It destroyed the first companion commit outright — recovered
  from the reflog. Push promptly; do not leave work local. Stage by path.
- **`sv` on the phone needs `SVDIR=$PREFIX/var/service`** or it reports every
  service missing. Logs are under `~/.local/state/sv-log/` and
  `$PREFIX/var/log/sv/`; `$LOGDIR` is unset.
- **Two listeners on 6601 is correct**: `mpv-music-bridge` on the Tailscale IP
  for red5, `mpv-music-bridge-local` on 127.0.0.1 for the app (dotfiles
  `a904216`). Never widen the tailnet one; it must not reach the wifi
  interface.
- **The phone backend expands no playlists.** `~/bin/play-local` passes
  `--no-playlist` deliberately, so build a queue with repeated `media music
  play <uri> --where phone --add`. Expect the occasional 403 and fetches past
  the 120 s default (`MEDIA_MUSIC_LOCAL_FETCH_TIMEOUT`); retries cleared both.
- **Our own speech will not trigger a focus loss.** sink-speech is also Termux
  mpv and requests no focus. A loss means a genuine outside app — a call, a
  navigation prompt. Worth confirming rather than assuming.
- Requesting `GAIN` tells other players to stop. That is the intent, but it is
  the first outward-facing behaviour change the app makes, so test it where a
  surprise is cheap.

## Verify before trusting

- A real phone call, with `call_guard` still live, to see whether the two
  cooperate or fight. Retiring `call_guard`'s music half is the follow-up, not
  part of this step.
- That taking focus does **not** silence the Termux mpv playing underneath.
  pulseaudio sits between them and has never been tested under a focus change.
  The spike never answered this: it held focus while producing no audio.
