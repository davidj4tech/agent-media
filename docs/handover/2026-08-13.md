## What is being built, in one paragraph

An Android companion app for p8a that publishes a **MediaSession** so earbud,
lock-screen and car-stereo controls reach agent-media, and owns **audio focus**
on mpv's behalf. It is not a player: Termux mpv keeps doing playback, keeping
the residential yt-dlp fetch, the offline cache, `--ao=pulse`, dynaudnorm and
the mpv JSON IPC contract `SinkMusicLocal` speaks. The app drives mpv over
**loopback TCP** and translates transport callbacks into mpv IPC commands.

## The amendment that cost three spike runs

On Android 17, an active `MediaSession` set to `STATE_PLAYING` does **not** win
the Bluetooth addressed-player slot. **An open audio stream is required.** The
app must hold an `AudioTrack` writing zeros for as long as it wants the
controls. Audio focus is not a substitute; neither is
`setMediaButtonBroadcastReceiver`. Both were tested alone and both failed.

Keep the track at full volume with an all-zero buffer — a zero-*volume* track
risks being optimised out of the mix, which would silently invalidate it.

Verified end to end on the real car: transport buttons work **and** the car
display shows the session metadata, while Termux mpv makes the actual sound.

## Constraints that will bite if forgotten

- **The mpv socket is unreachable from another app.** It lives inside
  `com.termux`'s private UID sandbox. Loopback TCP is the only route.
- **adb cannot reach p8a from red5.** adbd binds wlan0 only; loopback and the
  tailnet address are refused, Settings displays the (misleading) Tailscale IP,
  and the phone cannot reach its own wlan0 address while the VPN is up. Every
  install is a sideload: `scp` to `~/storage/downloads/` then
  `termux-open --chooser`. Every readout is on-screen or via a file Termux can
  read. A LAN machine would lift this; `pn` was not reachable.
- **Play Protect hard-blocks sideloaded apps declaring a
  `NotificationListenerService`**, with no install-anyway option. So
  `MediaSessionManager.getActiveSessions()` is unavailable — do not design
  around being able to enumerate sessions.
- **red5→p8a is ~1.1 s per round trip.** One snapshot call, and let the phone
  push state. Do not port the chatty property-at-a-time IPC to HTTP.
- **red5 is at ~89% disk.** The spike builds with `aapt2`/`javac`/`d8`/
  `apksigner` directly (`spikes/mediasession/build.sh`), no Gradle, no AndroidX,
  nothing from Maven. Keep it that way unless there is a real reason not to.

## First three steps

1. **A loopback listener for the mpv IPC socket.** The app needs
   `127.0.0.1:6601`. Add a **separate** runit service rather than editing
   `~/dotfiles/termux/services/mpv-music-bridge/run` — that one deliberately
   binds the Tailscale IP only, never the LAN interface, and carries a
   documented four-step address resolution because netlink is denied to the
   service's sandbox under runit. Do not disturb it.
2. **An mpv JSON IPC client in the app**, over that loopback port: send
   commands, read properties, observe `idle-active`/`pause`/`media-title`.
3. **Wire the session to mpv.** Transport callbacks become mpv commands;
   `media-title` becomes session metadata; the silent `AudioTrack` starts and
   stops with mpv's playback rather than with the app's lifetime, so it is not a
   permanent battery cost.

Then: audio focus (retiring the Automate mic-detect flag and simplifying
`call_guard`), and only after that the `/state` endpoint and push to red5.
