# Radio in the Media tab

Status: first version built 29 Sep 2026 (YouTube's Mix, 👎, Up next).
Date: 2026-09-29

David, 29 Sep 2026, from the Media tab with The Gambler playing: *"I think
I'd like a radio mode that plays a playlist of similar songs... how might
that look?"* After the sketch below: *"yes that sounds awesome"*, for the
first version (the Mix and 👎), with the Claude DJ left for later.

## What it looks like

```
▶ The Gambler — Kenny Rogers        1:04 / 3:31
⏮  ⏯  ⏭   ♡  ■  📻 Radio on  👎 Not this
── Up next · Radio from The Gambler ──
  Coward of the County      ✓
  Lucille                   …
  Daytime Friends
  +27 more
```

- **📻 Radio** on the playing track (a YouTube one) starts a station of songs
  like it. Tapped again (**Radio on**), it stops; what is playing plays on.
- **Up next** lists the songs to come: ✓ downloaded (queued in the player, or
  in the phone's cache), … downloading, then titles only.
- **👎 Not this** skips the song and keeps it off the station. A second 👎 on
  the same channel keeps the channel off. With no station, it is ⏭.
- **♡** is the like it always was, and also steers the station (below).
- Playing something else (a tap in Recently played, a chat asking for a song,
  `media music play`) ends the station; **add** to the queue does not.

## Where the songs come from

YouTube's **Mix**: every video has one (`watch?v=<id>&list=RD<id>`), the radio
YouTube itself plays after it. red5 is bot-walled, so the phone lists it —
`yt-dlp --flat-playlist`, ~5 s over ssh, nothing downloaded. For The Gambler
it is Kenny Rogers for a few songs, then John Denver, Glen Campbell, the
Highwaymen, Dolly Parton: a good "more like this" for no new account or key.

A Mix wanders after twenty songs or so. When fewer than five are left, the
station lists another: the Mix of the last song **liked** on the station,
else the last one played, each song's Mix listed once. Songs already
played or queued, 👎'd, struck channels, and the same title uploaded twice
("… (Official Audio)") are left out.

## Staying ahead

Every track is downloaded on the phone before it plays (play-local's
residential IP). So the station keeps:

1. the song playing,
2. **one queued** in the player behind it (`loadfile … append-play`; the
   queueing *is* the download), and
3. **one more in the phone's cache** (`play-local --fetch-only`), so a 👎 or
   ⏭ does not wait on YouTube.

A song the player will not take is tried three times (a slow phone refuses a
command as readily as YouTube refuses a download), then dropped. When the
player loses its song part-way — an app update restarts it empty (David, 29
Sep 2026, on the first station: "It got interrupted towards the end with an
update... continue from where it was at please") — the station puts it on
again where it was last seen, and what was queued behind it goes back to
the front of the list; a song that had played out is not put back, and
Stop ends the station so it is not. When a queued
song comes up, the station gives Sasonica's player its title, picture and
(no) chapters: that player keeps one of each and clears them only on a
replace.

## Who drives it

`agent_media_core/radio.py` is the station: one file (`radio.json` in the
state dir) read and written under a lock, and `tick()`, one look at the
player. `agent_media_server/radio.py` runs `tick()` every 4 s in the
canvas; an off station costs one small file read. The Media tab's start and
👎 wake it at once; `media music radio [URI|off]` and `media music dislike`
work from a shell (and so for an agent), and the loop picks up a station
started there.

Only the phone's players (Sasonica's own, the Termux mpv): both queue with
`append-play` and report `playlist-pos`. Mopidy has a YouTube radio of its
own, and is not this.

API: server-contract.md §6.9a "Radio".

## Claude DJ (built 29 Sep 2026)

A station whose songs a model picks for the moment (David: "Mood + moment"),
`agent_media_core/radio_dj.py`. The station is the same; only the source
differs. When the list runs low (and at the start) the DJ is shown:

- the moment as `astrotunes context` reads it — time of day, Melbourne's
  weather, the transits to David's chart as the six axes of the
  music-transit skill's brief (tempo, energy, warmth, brightness, lyric
  density, texture; amplified ones not pushed to extremes), the day's
  themes — kept half an hour;
- the last fifteen songs played, the next few to come, the likes, and the
  👎'd songs and struck artists to avoid;

and answers with six "Artist - Title" lines and a NOTE: on the mood, which
the Media tab can show. The phone finds each line on YouTube
(`ytsearch1:`), one ssh for all six. The model is MEDIA_RADIO_DJ_MODEL, else
the follow-up's (claude-haiku-4-5 through the gateway).

Slow: a pick took 50-105 s on 29 Sep 2026 (the model through Meridian, then
six searches), so a DJ station leaves what is playing on until its first
song is found and downloaded, then replaces it; after that it picks while a
song plays, as a Mix station tops up. The DJ is asked at most once a minute,
and a station whose DJ finds nothing three times turns off.

`POST /music {"action": "radio", "dj": true}`, `media music radio dj`.
